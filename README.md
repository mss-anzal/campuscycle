# CampusCycle

**Buy Smart. Sell Easy. Learn Better.**

## About the Project
CampusCycle is a student-to-student marketplace concept designed to help university students buy and sell affordable second-hand items within their campuses.

The project aims to make essential learning and personal items more accessible while encouraging reuse and reducing waste.

## Problem Statement
University students often struggle with the cost of new textbooks, stationery, electronics and other essential items. At the same time, many students have unused items they could sell.

## Our Solution
CampusCycle proposes a platform where students can connect, list items for sale and find affordable products within their university community.

## Target Market
- First-year university students
- Continuing students
- Final-year students
- Students selling unused items

## Proposed Features
- Student registration
- Product listings
- Search and browsing
- Product categories
- Student-to-student connections

## Business Model
Potential revenue sources include transaction fees, promoted listings and partnerships with campus businesses.

## Project Status
CampusCycle is currently at the idea and planning stage. The pitch deck presents the proposed business model and future development plans.

## Initial Target
Garissa University, Kenya, with potential expansion to other universities.
